SCENE VECTOR LIST — THE FEATURE RESEARCH BROUGHT UNDER SPECIFICATION

This list is the work that puts the scene-vector research under a
specification. The project measures what is on a HarmonyOS display, from
RenderService dumps, and asks whether that measurement carries information
about power draw and CPU frequency that the scheduler does not already
have. It reports structure. It decides no policy: aggregation, power
attribution and scheduling are elsewhere, and what is decided here is the
quality of the data they read.

Two things make this list what it is, and both were decided in the session
that preceded it. The first: there are no scene classes, and three attempts
at one were deleted — a C enum of twenty, a PowerShell classifier of
twenty-three, a naming function of twenty-eight shapes. The clustering is
not here to confirm a class list; it is given the vectors and nothing else.
The second: the spec graph is empty over 3,298 lines of tracked code, so
the governance adopted in 2da1e4d has nothing to verify with. Every item
below exists to close one of those two gaps or to repair something the
audits found while looking.

Each item is one skill execution, run in the order below.

- /cb-audit executions read and report; they change nothing.
- /cb-fix executions repair a defect against a reproduction.
- /cb-free sessions bring each finding to the human, one at a time, and
  write the decision into THE DECISIONS IN FORCE of this list.
- /cb-green executions author spec nodes and change code.

Every item ends by bringing this list to the state its work leaves, in the
same session, before the gate is cleared:
- the item is deleted, with its line under What remains, and its prompt;
- each record of THE DECISIONS IN FORCE the item applies is written again to
  what is still to do, its "Applied by" line naming only the items left, and
  deleted when no item is left;
- each finding the item reports and does not resolve is written as an open
  point of the record it bears on when it is a question for the human, and
  as an item in its place in the order, with its prompt, when it is work;
- each name the item wrote differently from the record is written into the
  record and into every later item that cites it, in the form the item
  wrote;
- every reference in the list to the deleted item, or to a name it changed,
  is written again or removed.
An audit cannot write; the list update an audit owes is done by the /cb-free
session after it, which names it in its report.

An item pushes before its gate is cleared. Authoring happens on the VM and
the list is read on the capture machine, so a committed-but-unpushed update
leaves the next item to be chosen from a list that is behind the work. That
happened once: the first item was executed, deleted from this list, and
committed, and because nothing was pushed the same item was read and run a
second time against a graph that already held its nodes. The item is not
finished when the list is written; it is finished when the list the human
reads says so.

Every prompt carries a Spec work section naming its specification work node
by node: each node it adds (id, kind, module, and every edge it carries),
each node it modifies (id and the fields written), each node and each edge
it removes. An item that changes no node says so and gives the reason. The
names a prompt gives are record 5's; the human confirms them before the
envelope is written.

Every prompt carries the BOUNDARY clause: combobul and ArkUI are two
projects, and no rule, kind, check or store of one is carried to the other.

An item runs, at its end, the checks that read the files it changed, and
names them in its report: `npm run spec:check`, and the pipeline on a real
capture where its change reaches the pipeline.

A measurement that an item claims is verified is verified in that session,
against a real capture, and the figures are written into the report. A
figure carried from an earlier session is marked as such.

The captures carry no weight on the design: they are two sessions of one
device held by one person, and nothing in this list treats a number taken
from them as settled beyond the capture it came from. Captures live on the
capture machine and never in this repository — they are pictures of a real
phone in real use.

The baseline the executions read (read each file in full):

- tools/rs_capture.ps1 (424) — the device side.
- tools/correlate.py (379), tools/sample_power.sh (64) — the open question.
- CLAUDE.md — the governance in force.

THE DECISIONS IN FORCE
===============================================================================

  Each record states a decision the items below carry out. Delete a record
  when every item it names is done.

  1. The scope. Decided by the human.
       * The project measures the structure of what is on a display and
         asks whether that measurement predicts power and frequency. It
         reports composition; it decides no policy.
       * The eventual deliverable is a system component: ArkUI is platform
         code, so one patch reaches every app through the pre-warmed
         appspawn fork. That component does not exist and this repository
         is not it. What exists is a capture, two measured vectors, a
         clustering, a page, and an unanswered question.
       * The research is one mechanism: PowerShell stops at the device
         boundary and Python owns everything after it. The device side
         derives no feature and names nothing; it drives hdc, decides from
         the raw text whether a scene has been seen, and writes files.
       * The human cannot run the agent on the capture machine. Authoring
         happens on the VM; the capture machine is a clone that pulls, runs
         the capture and runs the pipeline against its own data.

     Applied by: every item.

  2. The two vectors and their column contracts. Decided by the human.
       * The pattern vector — what repeats in the layout, and how much of
         the window it holds. Branches are grouped by a description of
         their subtree, and the group covering the most area wins. Ties
         break on vertical position, never on the nearly-tied area.

           column         transform  weight  measures
           pat1_cover     unit       1.0     share of the window that repeats
           pat1_h         unit       1.0     the item's height, in display heights
           pat1_aspect    logratio   1.0     its width / height
           pat1_cols      clip6      1.2     distinct columns
           pat1_n         log1p      0.7     members in the group
           overlay_frac   unit       0.7     area drawn over the layout
           pat_n_groups   log1p      0.7     how many repeating groups exist

       * The surface vector — what the display is finally made of, after
         occlusion. Every node that draws, and every externally composed
         surface, walked topmost first, each claiming only the pixels
         nothing above it claimed. Substrate is the complement.

           column         transform  weight  measures
           vis_xcomp      unit       1.0     externally composed surface
           vis_image      unit       1.0     nodes tagged Image
           vis_text       unit       1.0     nodes tagged Text
           vis_content    unit       0.7     draws, but no tag names the type
           vis_substrate  unit       0.7     claimed by nothing

       * A second pattern group is measured into pat2_* with has_slot2 and
         is not in the vector. pat, arr, cols, rows stay as measurements —
         content-kind letters off node tags, geometric arrangement, column
         count — and are not matches against a library.
       * Both are measurements. Neither is a class.
       * Every tuned constant now states its contract as a precondition of
         the algorithm that reads it: what it separates, what changing it
         breaks, and that all of them were fitted by sweep on the two
         sessions of 2026-10-22, 250 scenes of one device held by one
         person, and have not been re-fitted since. Fourteen were expected
         and ten were found: COINCIDENT, OVERLAY_IN, OVERLAY_MAX, POS_TOL,
         BUDGET, OVERSIZE, SURF_MIN, FULLBLEED_H, SURF_BANDS and
         SLOT_CONTENT, plus the capture's Tol and ShotEvery and the
         clustering's min_cluster_size and THUMB_W.
       * Four were dead. SLOT_CHILDREN, SLOTS_BRACKET, SLOTS_BACKGROUND and
         SLOTS_BORDER were defined and never read - the working notes of
         the bracket-pair derivation, left behind when the substrate rule
         replaced them. They are deleted; the derivation and the slot-50
         caveat live in ALG-VISIBLE-SURFACE's contract beside SLOT_CONTENT,
         which is the one slot still read. Nothing referenced them, so the
         output over all 250 scenes is byte-identical.
       * patterns.csv is 61 columns wide, of which these 12 are the vectors
         and 4 identify the scene. The other 45 are measured and carried for
         inspection, not clustered. IF-PATTERNS-ROW names every one and says
         which is which; this record states the vectors alone, and the node
         is where the full width is settled.

     Applied by: the surface vector's collapse; the diagnosis brought to
     the human.

  3. What is measured and what is not. Decided by the evidence; verified
     2026-10-26 against the two capture sessions, 250 scenes.
       * Holds, and is now INV-SURFACE-SUMS-TO-ONE: the six surface columns
         depart from one by at most 0.0060 over 250 rows, against a
         tolerance of 0.01 that exists for the rounding of six numbers
         written to three decimals. The five in the vector depart by at
         most 0.0060 as well, because vis_glyph - the one they leave out -
         never exceeds 0.0060 on this capture. Six is the number that sums
         exactly: visible_surface claims pixels for xcomp, image, text,
         glyph and content, and substrate is what none of them claimed.
       * Holds, and is now INV-UNIT-COLUMNS-BOUNDED: nine columns are
         declared unit - overlay_frac, pat1_cover, pat1_h and the six vis_*
         - and over 250 rows none leaves [0,1]. pat1_cover and pat1_h carry
         28 empty fields, which is a measurement not taken and is allowed.
       * Holds, and is now INV-DETERMINISTIC-MEASUREMENT: a session measured
         three times in separate processes under PYTHONHASHSEED 1, 7 and
         4242 gives one sha256. Checked across hash seeds rather than by
         running twice in one interpreter, because the realistic way for it
         to break is a set or dict iteration order reaching the output and
         two runs in one process share a seed.
       * Did not hold, and does now: the slot ordering sorted on the
         unclamped coverage while pat1_cover reports min(1.0, ratio). On
         20261022_123014/0090 two groups both overflowed the window, both
         reported 1.0, and the lower one took slot 1 because it overflowed
         further - so the file could not explain its own ordering. The sort
         clamps now, which is INV-TIEBREAK-NOT-ON-AREA. One row of 250
         changed; the pattern silhouette moved 0.467 to 0.468.
       * Holds, and is now INV-ABSENT-NOT-ZERO: those same 28 scenes have
         no repeating group and none of them writes a zero. transform('')
         and transform(None) are nan, transform('0') is 0.0, and a nan
         column is filled with its mean. The rule spans two files and now
         has a check that spans them too.
       * Holds: the reduction of rs_extract.py from 1,028 lines to 404 left
         the output byte-identical on ten real dumps. Determinism is what
         made that reduction checkable rather than arguable.
       * Does not hold: 71 of 250 measured scenes never reach the page.
         Every number the page publishes — 6 clusters, 11 percent fitting
         none, silhouette 0.499 at min_cluster_size 9 — is over the
         remaining 179 and over an undeclared subset.
       * Corrected 2026-10-26, by the item that repaired it. This record
         said the cause was an addressing mistake and that all 250 scenes
         had a picture. Both were wrong, and so was the measurement behind
         them: the check joined <captures> to an empty shot column, which
         yields the captures directory, and a directory exists - so 71
         empty strings counted as 71 present files. The truth is that 179
         of 250 scenes have a per-scene picture and 71 have none, because
         the capture photographs a scene when a pair settles and 71 never
         settled. Being dropped from the run entirely was the defect; they
         are now measured, clustered and shown with an empty tile.
       * The capture does hold pictures for them. It writes periodic shots
         named p<nnnnnn>.jpeg - 169 across the two sessions - which no
         filename ties to a scene, and samples.csv relates a stem to the
         shot taken for it. The index exists and nothing reads it.
       * What the repair changed, over the full capture: the pattern vector
         goes from 6 clusters, 19 fitting none (11%) and silhouette 0.499
         over 179 scenes, to 6 clusters, 27 fitting none (11%) and
         silhouette 0.467 over 250. The surface vector goes from 4
         clusters, 42 fitting none (23%) and silhouette 0.455, to 7
         clusters, 106 fitting none (42%) and silhouette 0.665. A vector
         that degrades as scenes are added is the collapse below showing
         itself, not a result.
       * Does not hold: the surface vector collapses towards a single axis
         on this capture, substrate against content at about -0.8, and
         clusters at 23 percent outliers with a silhouette of 0.455 that
         rewards a split a person would not make. It separates an external
         surface holding the display from everything else, which is real.
         The rest is not settled.

     Applied by: the 71 scenes given a picture; the surface vector's
