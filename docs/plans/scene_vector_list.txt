SCENE VECTOR LIST — THE FEATURE RESEARCH BROUGHT UNDER SPECIFICATION

This list is the work that puts the scene-vector research under a
specification. The project does two things: it collects ArkUI data off a
HarmonyOS device, and it clusters the scenes it finds in that data. What is
decided here is the quality of that collection and that clustering.

Two things make this list what it is, and both were decided in the session
that preceded it. The first: there are no scene classes, and three attempts
at one were deleted — a C enum of twenty, a PowerShell classifier of
twenty-three, a naming function of twenty-eight shapes. The clustering is
not here to confirm a class list; it is given the vectors and nothing else.
The second: the spec graph is empty over 3,298 lines of tracked code, so
the governance adopted in 2da1e4d has nothing to verify with. Every item
below exists to close one of those two gaps or to repair something the
audits found while looking.

Each item is one skill execution, run in the order below.

- /cb-audit executions read and report; they change nothing.
- /cb-fix executions repair a defect against a reproduction.
- /cb-free sessions bring each finding to the human, one at a time, and
  write the decision into THE DECISIONS IN FORCE of this list.
- /cb-green executions author spec nodes and change code.

Every item ends by bringing this list to the state its work leaves, in the
same session, before the gate is cleared:
- the item is deleted, with its line under What remains, and its prompt;
- the item is appended to WHAT IS DONE, with its date, its commit and what
  it actually found - including when what it found is that the item itself
  was wrong. Deleting an item without recording it leaves a list that shows
  only what is left, and a reader of that list cannot tell work that was
  done from work that was dropped;
- each record of THE DECISIONS IN FORCE the item applies is written again to
  what is still to do, its "Applied by" line naming only the items left, and
  deleted when no item is left;
- each finding the item reports and does not resolve is written as an open
  point of the record it bears on when it is a question for the human, and
  as an item in its place in the order, with its prompt, when it is work;
- each name the item wrote differently from the record is written into the
  record and into every later item that cites it, in the form the item
  wrote;
- every reference in the list to the deleted item, or to a name it changed,
  is written again or removed.
An audit cannot write; the list update an audit owes is done by the /cb-free
session after it, which names it in its report.

An item pushes before its gate is cleared. Authoring happens on the VM and
the list is read on the capture machine, so a committed-but-unpushed update
leaves the next item to be chosen from a list that is behind the work. That
happened once: the first item was executed, deleted from this list, and
committed, and because nothing was pushed the same item was read and run a
second time against a graph that already held its nodes. The item is not
finished when the list is written; it is finished when the list the human
reads says so.

Every prompt carries a Spec work section naming its specification work node
by node: each node it adds (id, kind, module, and every edge it carries),
each node it modifies (id and the fields written), each node and each edge
it removes. An item that changes no node says so and gives the reason. The
names a prompt gives are record 4's; the human confirms them before the
envelope is written.

Every prompt carries the BOUNDARY clause: combobul and ArkUI are two
projects, and no rule, kind, check or store of one is carried to the other.

An item runs, at its end, the checks that read the files it changed, and
names them in its report: `npm run spec:check`, and the pipeline on a real
capture where its change reaches the pipeline.

A measurement that an item claims is verified is verified in that session,
against a real capture, and the figures are written into the report. A
figure carried from an earlier session is marked as such.

The captures carry no weight on the design: they are two sessions of one
device held by one person, and nothing in this list treats a number taken
from them as settled beyond the capture it came from. Captures live on the
capture machine and never in this repository — they are pictures of a real
phone in real use.

The baseline the executions read (read each file in full):
- README.md, tools/README.md, tools/cluster/README.md — what the project is
  and what each stage does.
- tools/cluster/rs_extract.py (404 lines), rs_pattern.py (795),
  vectors_build.py (209), vectors_page.py (137) — the measurement.
- tools/rs_capture.ps1 (424) — the device side.
- CLAUDE.md — the governance in force.
- .combobul/runs/audit-spec-genesis/contract.json and
  .combobul/runs/audit-subfeature-spec/contract.json — the two audits this
  list carries out, findings F1–F9 and S1–S11 with their classifications.

THE DECISIONS IN FORCE
===============================================================================

  Each record states a decision the items below carry out. Delete a record
  when every item it names is done.

  1. The scope. Decided by the human, restated 2026-10-27.
       * This project collects ArkUI data and clusters the scenes in it.
         That is the whole of it.
       * Nothing else goes in: no measurement of anything the device does
         in response to a scene, no joining of a scene to any other series,
         and no code whose reason for existing is a question somebody
         downstream of this data would ask.
       * The test for a proposed item is whether it improves the collection
         or the clustering. If it does not, it does not belong here, and no
         item in this list can widen that.
       * What exists is a capture, two measured vectors, a clustering and
         a page.
       * The research is one mechanism: PowerShell stops at the device
         boundary and Python owns everything after it. The device side
         derives no feature and names nothing; it drives hdc, decides from
         the raw text whether a scene has been seen, and writes files.
       * The human cannot run the agent on the capture machine. Authoring
         happens on the VM; the capture machine is a clone that pulls, runs
         the capture and runs the pipeline against its own data.

     Applied by: every item.

  2. The two vectors and their column contracts. Decided by the human.
       * The pattern vector — what repeats in the layout, and how much of
         the window it holds. Branches are grouped by a description of
         their subtree, and the group covering the most area wins. Ties
         break on vertical position, never on the nearly-tied area.

           column         transform  weight  measures
           pat1_cover     unit       1.0     share of the window that repeats
           pat1_h         unit       1.0     the item's height, in display heights
           pat1_aspect    logratio   1.0     its width / height
           pat1_cols      clip6      1.2     distinct columns
           pat1_n         log1p      0.7     members in the group
           overlay_frac   unit       0.7     area drawn over the layout
           pat_n_groups   log1p      0.7     how many repeating groups exist

       * The surface vector — what the display is finally made of, after
         occlusion. Every node that draws, and every externally composed
         surface, walked topmost first, each claiming only the pixels
         nothing above it claimed. Substrate is the complement.

           column         transform  weight  measures
           vis_xcomp      unit       1.0     externally composed surface
           vis_image      unit       1.0     nodes tagged Image
           vis_text       unit       1.0     nodes tagged Text
           vis_content    unit       0.7     draws, but no tag names the type
           vis_substrate  unit       0.7     claimed by nothing

         vis_unnamed is written beside them and is NOT a term of the
         partition: it is the part of vis_content claimed by nodes with no
         client-dump entry, carried so that content nobody can name is
         distinguishable from content a mapping failed to cover. It is a
         subset of vis_content and is never added to the sum.

       * A second pattern group is measured into pat2_* with has_slot2 and
         is not in the vector. pat, arr, cols, rows stay as measurements —
         content-kind letters off node tags, geometric arrangement, column
         count — and are not matches against a library.
       * Both are measurements. Neither is a class.
       * Every tuned constant now states its contract as a precondition of
         the algorithm that reads it: what it separates, what changing it
         breaks, and that all of them were fitted by sweep on the two
         sessions of 2026-10-22, 250 scenes of one device held by one
         person, and have not been re-fitted since. Fourteen were expected
         and ten were found: COINCIDENT, OVERLAY_IN, OVERLAY_MAX, POS_TOL,
         BUDGET, OVERSIZE, SURF_MIN, FULLBLEED_H, SURF_BANDS and
         SLOT_CONTENT, plus the capture's Tol and the clustering's
         min_cluster_size and THUMB_W. ShotEvery was among them and is gone:
         it named samples in one place and seconds in another, and the clock
         it drove was deleted on 2026-10-27.
       * Four were dead. SLOT_CHILDREN, SLOTS_BRACKET, SLOTS_BACKGROUND and
         SLOTS_BORDER were defined and never read - the working notes of
         the bracket-pair derivation, left behind when the substrate rule
         replaced them. They are deleted; the derivation and the slot-50
         caveat live in ALG-VISIBLE-SURFACE's contract beside SLOT_CONTENT,
         which is the one slot still read. Nothing referenced them, so the
         output over all 250 scenes is byte-identical.
       * patterns.csv is 61 columns wide, of which these 12 are the vectors
         and 4 identify the scene. The other 45 are measured and carried for
         inspection, not clustered. IF-PATTERNS-ROW names every one and says
         which is which; this record states the vectors alone, and the node
         is where the full width is settled.


     Applied by: the two feature spaces formalized; PSI for the pattern
     vector; PSI for the surface vector.

  6. The two mappings, and what a pattern set must satisfy. Decided by the
     human, 2026-10-27.
       * The pipeline is two mappings, not one:

             component tree --extract--> measurements --PHI--> feature vector
             feature vector --PSI--> pattern

         PHI exists and is rs_pattern.py. PSI does not: it was deleted along
         with the asserted name tables and nothing replaced it. That is why
         both vectors stop at stage 1, why "which pattern is this scene" has
         no answer, and why the page could only ever report clusters.
       * PSI is DERIVED, never asserted. The deleted sets - a C enum of 20,
         a PowerShell classifier of 23, a naming function of 28 shapes, a
         DESC table of 13 - were lists written first and matched against the
         data afterwards. The pattern set is instead derived from the
         structure of the measurement space, so that it can be written down
         without looking at a single screenshot. That is the test which
         keeps it a function rather than a class list, and it is why
         INV-NO-SCENE-CLASS permits it: a partition of a space is a
         function; a library of named shapes matched against data is not.
       * PSI must be demonstrated to be all four of:
           total       every feature vector maps to exactly one pattern. A
                       scene fits nothing only by landing in a declared
                       cell, never by falling through.
           disjoint    no feature vector maps to two.
           minimal     no two patterns are indistinguishable under PHI, and
                       no cell is empty by construction.
           orthogonal  the axes defining the cells are independent, so a
                       pattern is a point in a product rather than an
                       overlapping description.
         A demonstration is the deliverable. An implementation whose mapping
         is not shown to have all four has not done this work.
       * The spaces have shapes, and the formalization starts from them:
           surface   a simplex. Five non-negative columns summing to one, so
                     the space is the standard 4-simplex. A partition of a
                     simplex by dominant component and concentration is
                     exhaustive and disjoint by construction. "What can be
                     expected from a composition" is then the question of
                     which cells are reachable, which the data answers
                     rather than the author.
           pattern   a product. arrangement is one axis and is closed
                     because arrangement() can emit nothing but grid, row,
                     column and stack; content signature is a second; scale
                     and coverage bands a third. The pattern set is the
                     realizable subset of that product.
       * The number is not the target. The human recalls about 16 patterns
         in the deleted set, and four arrangements by four signature classes
         is also 16. That coincidence is worth noticing and is not evidence.
         Whether that factorization is the right one is precisely what the
         formalization has to establish.
       * Orthogonality is also the test ACROSS the two vectors. If PSI of
         the pattern vector and PSI of the surface vector produce outcomes
         that are independent, the two vectors are measuring different
         things and the joint view is worth building. If they do not, that
         is the finding.
       * FORMALIZED 2026-10-27. docs/design/feature-spaces.md carries the
         reasoning; IF-PATTERN-SPACE, IF-SURFACE-SPACE, ALG-PSI-PATTERN,
         ALG-PSI-SURFACE and INV-PSI-{TOTAL,DISJOINT,MINIMAL,ORTHOGONAL}
         carry the contract. The two algorithms are DECLARED and not
         implemented; the two items after this one write them.
       * PSI_p = (arrangement, whether the group covers more than half the
         window), plus one cell for a scene with no group. Nine cells, nine
         reachable: grid 47/46, row 1/87, column 5/78, stack 1/3, none 31.
         One half earns its place here - a group covering more than half IS
         the screen's layout and one covering less is an element inside
         another - and the arrangement axis is closed because arrangement()
         can emit nothing but those four.
       * PSI_s = (largest coordinate, second largest), vis_glyph excluded,
         NO threshold anywhere. Twenty cells, sixteen reachable, largest
         cell 23 percent. The rejected alternative was (argmax, above one
         half): 12 cells, 8 reachable, and one cell holding 179 of 299
         scenes, which separates almost nothing. Exactly one scene ties for
         the largest coordinate, so the coordinate order is declared rather
         than left to the sort.
       * INV-PSI-MINIMAL DOES NOT HOLD for the surface space. Four of its
         twenty cells are empty and they are exactly the row where vis_text
         leads: text is never the largest coordinate in any of 299 scenes.
         Either no text-dominant screen exists on this device and these
         applications - two sessions of one person's phone is a thin basis
         for believing it - or the measurement cannot express one because
         text sits inside containers whose paint takes the pixels first.
         The second is testable by capturing a reader or a document editor,
         and that test is not scheduled by this list.
       * The weakest step, recorded as such: vis_glyph is excluded as a
         ranking candidate on an argument from the component - a glyph and
         a hairline are bounded small by what they are - not on a proof. It
         is the first thing to revisit if a device is captured where a
         glyph fills a display.

     Applied by: PSI for the pattern vector; PSI for the surface vector;
     the viewers.
