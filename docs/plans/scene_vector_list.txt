SCENE VECTOR LIST — WHAT IS TO BE DONE

The project collects ArkUI data from a HarmonyOS device and describes the
scene on the display. This list holds the work still to be done, in order,
and nothing else. What was done, and why, is in git and in the transcripts.

HOW THE LIST WORKS

- ORDER holds one line per item, in the order they run.
- PROMPTS holds each item's full prompt, starting "/cb-green — <title>",
  "/cb-fix — <title>" or "/cb-audit — <title>" with no prefix, ready to
  paste.
- DEFERRED holds items kept for later, with their prompts. They are not run
  until the human moves them into ORDER.
- Each item is one skill execution.

When an item is finished, in the same session, before its gate is cleared:
- its line is deleted from ORDER and its prompt from PROMPTS;
- every later item it changes is written again to match;
- work it found and did not do is written as a new item in ORDER, with its
  prompt, or as an open point inside the item it belongs to;
- the list is committed, and the push is made through /cb-ops.

RULES FOR EVERY ITEM

- Specification first. Every node an item adds, modifies or removes is
  authored through ril before the code it governs. A removal of an
  invariant is confirmed by the human, node by node, before the envelope.
- No scene classes. A motif names a structure of components; nothing in
  the repository maps a measurement onto the name of a scene, an app or a
  use.
- Only what is visible on the display enters a feature.
- Terms: the display is the coordinate space every area and fraction is
  measured in; a window is one top-level surface, and several coexist on a
  display; the screen is the physical panel, and is used only for motion.
- Sample timing is read from the capture's samples.csv, never from file
  times.
- Every figure an item reports is measured in that session on a real
  capture. npm run spec:check passes, and the checks that read the changed
  files are run and named.
- Before committing spec work: npm run ril -- journal coalesce.
- BOUNDARY: combobul and ArkUI are two projects. combobul/spec/spec.db holds
  the specification of this project's code, written only through ril. No
  rule, kind, check or store of one is carried to the other.
- The repository lives only on the VM. The capture machine reads it as a
  read-only share and runs the tools from ~\ark_work, where the captures,
  the venv and every output stay. Captures never enter the repository.

The baseline each item reads in full: README.md, tools/README.md, CLAUDE.md,
tools/rs_capture.ps1, tools/cluster/rs_extract.py, rs_scene.py,
rs_pattern.py, vectors_build.py, vectors_page.py, and the checks under
tools/checks/.

/cb-green — the capture: every sample a bracketed bundle
  - What: rs_capture.ps1 stops judging scenes. Every sample is one bundle,
    read back to back in this order: the render tree (before), the
    screenshot, the client dump, the SceneBoard inspector, the render tree
    again (after). The shot sits next to the tree it is paired with, and
    both tag dumps sit inside the bracket, so a sample whose before and
    after trees agree held still across all five. Every attempt writes a
    row; nothing is compared, deduplicated or skipped live. The pipeline
    reads only this format: a sample is a scene when its bundle is complete
    and its before and after trees keep the same display, and its tags
    come from its own two dumps. The old sessions, and the code that reads
    them, go.
  - DECIDED by the human on 2026-10-30: a full bundle every sample (about
    1.3 s a sample, against 0.3 s now); the tree read again after the
    shot, the pairing within the sample; only the new format accepted, the
    old sessions wiped; pictures pulled at the end, in a finally; the
    confirmed cases of scene_pass.py confirmed again on the new capture.
  - WHY, from the review of 2026-10-30 (read-only, every session of
    2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28):
      the picture rule decides the dataset and rests on an unsound
        signature: 572 pictures in 7,292 samples, chosen by every node's
        innerAbsDrawRect over the whole dump, first match at 650 permille;
        its tuning variables (SigGrid, SigBigArea, ScreenW, ScreenH) are
        read and never set, so its "big rect" branch never runs;
      tags are borrowed: the client dump is read only for a sample judged
        new, and 147 of the 342 scenes are under half tagged, the TikTok
        clusters at about 0.09;
      the system UI is untagged: WindowManagerService -w <id> -inspector
        names SceneBoard's components, and its compid equals the render
        node's nodeName on 93 of 179 launcher render nodes (cb-audit of
        2026-10-30); the client dump cannot bridge it;
      the shot is not bracketed: 201 of 543 pictures refused as "display
        changed" against the next sample;
      pictures can be lost: they wait on the device for one transfer after
        the loop, Ctrl+C skips it, and the next session starts with rm -rf
        on the folder; 20261027_072726 names 55 and has 26;
      failures are silent: hdc errors swallowed, a failed sample counted
        and never written, no dump checked for completeness;
      timing is coarse and host-side: ts written after the sample, ms the
        total, no device time;
      dead and stale: dwell.csv has no reader; comments name -RsRebuildAll
        and "the two vectors";
      display.txt is read once a session, and its absence is silently
        replaced by a default panel (DISPLAY_DEFAULT); ERR-DISPLAY-DUMP-
        MISSING and ERR-CLIENT-DUMP-MISSING are raised by no code;
      INV-NO-CAPTURES-TRACKED says .gitignore excludes .rstree.txt,
        .client.txt, display.txt and samples.csv; it names none of them
        (captures/ covers the folder), and boundary_rules.py checks only
        *.jpeg, *.png and captures/.
  - MEASURE FIRST, read-only on the device, before the envelope; the
    figures are put to the human:
      1. the SceneBoard inspector: whether one window's -inspector answers
         for every SceneBoard window on the display (desktop, status bar,
         gesture layers) or only its own; whether the window ids hold
         across a session; the cost of each read. The human decides which
         inspector reads the bundle makes and how their window ids are
         found.
      2. the device clock read in the same hdc shell command as a step:
         its resolution and what it adds.
      3. whether the before tree's LOGICAL_DISPLAY_NODE has display.txt's
         size on every sample of the capture of 2026-10-28.
  - THE SESSION (IF-CAPTURE-SESSION, rewritten):
      <session>/display.txt, read once, at the start;
      <session>/samples.csv, one row per attempt, in order: sample
        (s000001, ...), status (ok, or the step that failed with hdc's own
        text), host_start, dev_before, dev_shot, dev_after (the device
        clock, read in the step's own shell command), ms_before, ms_shot,
        ms_client, ms_inspector, ms_after;
      per sample: <sample>.before.rstree.txt, <sample>.jpeg,
        <sample>.client.txt, <sample>.inspector.txt,
        <sample>.after.rstree.txt.
    A failed step ends its sample, and its row is still written. Pictures
    are written on the device to the session's own folder,
    /data/local/tmp/arkshots/<session>, pulled in one transfer in a
    finally (it runs on Ctrl+C), each one an ok row names checked on the
    host, and the device folder removed only when every one is there.
    Nothing is wiped at the start; a folder an earlier session left is
    named.
  - Spec work (node-info and impact-analysis on every node named, before
    the envelope; each removal named to the human before the envelope; no
    invariant is removed):
      REMOVE
        ALG-QUICK-SIG - nothing compares samples live.
        ALG-TAG-BORROW - a sample's tags are its own.
        ERR-CLIENT-DUMP-MISSING, ERR-DISPLAY-DUMP-MISSING - raised by no
          code; a missing dump is now an incomplete sample or a malformed
          session.
      ADD
        algorithm ALG-SAMPLE-TAGS (py; runs-at AUT-PIPELINE-RUN; called by
          ALG-PICTURE-PAIR; IF-CAPTURE-SESSION input-of it): a render
          node's tag is its frameNodeTag in the sample's own client dump,
          by node id; a node with none there takes the tag of the component

          names its samples, which ALG-PICTURE-PAIR loses with their
          reason. Emits ERR-SESSION-MALFORMED (new, ERROR). Verified by a

      MODIFY
        IF-CAPTURE-SESSION - the session above; input-of ALG-SAMPLE-TAGS;
          assumes INV-SESSION-COMPLETE.
        ALG-CAPTURE-LOOP - the bundle, the rows, the transfer. Precondition:
          a shot command answered (ERR-SHOT-COMMAND-ABSENT). Postcondition:
          when the loop ends, on time or on Ctrl+C, every attempt has its
          row, and every picture an ok row names is on the host, or named
          and still in the session's folder on the device.
        AUT-CAPTURE-RUN and INV-DEVICE-COMPUTES-NOTHING - the device side
          drives hdc and writes files, and decides nothing about a scene.
          Predicate: rs_capture.ps1 names no vector column and defines no
          function that measures, compares, classifies or names a scene -
          no signature, no tolerance, no index of scenes - and its rows
          carry exactly IF-CAPTURE-SESSION's columns (boundary_rules.py).
        ALG-PICTURE-PAIR - every row is a candidate. It is a scene when its
          status is ok, its five files are there, its before tree has one

          before and after trees keeps the same nodes, every visible cell

          sample" goes. Postcondition: rows of samples.csv == scenes +
          lost.
        INV-PICTURE-SAME-MOMENT - a scene's five files are from one sample,
