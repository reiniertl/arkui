What is measured

A scene is the set of rendered nodes, from any window on a display, whose clipped rectangle survives occlusion, in that display's coordinates. It is defined at an instant; the change against the previous reading is treated as a modifier, never as part of the class.

Every number comes from RenderService's own tree. The accessibility tree is not read at all. Joining the render tree to the component tree succeeds on 100% of nodes, against 38% for the accessibility route, and it costs one dump instead of two.

Three corrections that mattered

The shape of what repeats is now a distribution, not a winner. Four features used to come from whichever repeating group covered the most area. A screen normally has several at once, so the rest was discarded, and when two were close the pick alternated between samples — one screen crossed between two classes sixteen times without changing. Every group now contributes to five height bands and three aspect bands, weighted by area. Clusters went from 11 at silhouette 0.426 to 14 at 0.477.

Six features were repeating each other. Three of them measured one axis under three names and carried 47% of all distance between scenes. Removing the duplicates raised separation and cost nothing.

Deduplication no longer depends on the features. It used to measure distance in the same space being tuned, so every feature change quietly redrew the dataset. The rule is now stated and frozen: four categorical fields must match exactly, the rest within a stated tolerance. Three different feature sets now produce byte-identical datasets.

What this does not yet support

About a third of scenes fit no class, and that number is stable. 92 of 324 measurements are unassigned at every setting tried. They are not noise and not members — they sit on the slope between classes.

Density clustering reports what it cannot place rather than forcing it. That is the correct behaviour for discovering classes and the wrong behaviour for labelling every frame.

The next step is the one this work exists to set up: train a classifier on the confident members, which assigns every scene including those on the boundary. Clustering discovers the classes; it should not be asked to serve them.

Honest limits

  • One device, one session, a single application for most of it. The six classes are what this session contains, not a taxonomy.
  • Roughly half of samples have no nearby screenshot, so visual checking covers part of the set.
  • The distribution change improved what the descriptor says; it did not make it steadier between consecutive samples, which is what it was intended to fix.
  • Scene classes are reported here. Aggregation, power attribution and scheduling policy are decided elsewhere, from this input.
